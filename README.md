# Two-fuild-TOV-Equation
本仓库提供了一个双流体Tolman-Oppenheimer-Volkoff（TOV）方程的数值求解库，专门用于研究暗物质-中子星混合系统的复杂物理过程。该模拟框架旨在探索暗物质与致密天体相互作用的多种可能场景，并为相关天体物理现象提供数值解法和分析工具。

双流体TOV方程扩展了经典中子星结构模型，通过引入暗物质流体组分与核物质流体组分的耦合，能够自洽地描述暗物质在中子星内部的分布及其对恒星整体结构的影响。这一框架对于理解暗物质与普通物质在极端引力环境下的相互作用至关重要，特别是在多信使天文学时代，中子星作为宇宙中极端的物理实验室，为探索非标准模型物理提供了独特窗口。

暗物质-中子星混合系统的研究具有多重科学意义。一方面，中子星可能成为暗物质的有效俘获器，其内部积累的暗物质会修改恒星的状态方程，进而影响质量-半径关系、潮汐形变能力和旋转动力学。另一方面，这种混合系统可能产生独特的观测特征，如修改后的中子星冷却曲线、异常的脉冲星计时行为以及引力波信号的畸变。此外，通过数值模拟探索暗物质在中子星内部的相变、凝聚和自相互作用等效应，能够为暗物质粒子性质提供重要的天体物理约束。

本数值模拟库旨在构建一个灵活、高效的计算平台，持续集成和开发针对双流体TOV方程的各种数值解法。我们将不断更新包括但不限于：不同暗物质状态方程的实现、耦合流体方程的数值稳定性处理、相对论性多流体系统的守恒格式设计、以及考虑旋转和磁场扩展的高维求解器。通过开源这一工具集，我们希望促进理论天体物理、粒子物理和数值相对论领域的交叉合作，为理解暗物质本质和中子星内部结构提供可靠的计算基础。

This repository offers a numerical solution library for the two-fluid Tolman-Oppenheimer-Volkoff (TOV) equation, specifically designed to study the complex physical processes in dark matter-neutron star hybrid systems. The simulation framework aims to explore various possible scenarios of the interaction between dark matter and compact objects and provide numerical solutions and analytical tools for related astrophysical phenomena. The two-fluid TOV equation extends the classical neutron star structure model by introducing the coupling between the dark matter fluid component and the nuclear matter fluid component, enabling a self-consistent description of the distribution of dark matter within neutron stars and its impact on the overall stellar structure. This framework is crucial for understanding the interaction between dark matter and ordinary matter in extreme gravitational environments, especially in the era of multi-messenger astronomy, where neutron stars, as extreme physical laboratories in the universe, offer a unique window into exploring physics beyond the standard model. 

The study of dark matter-neutron star hybrid systems holds multiple scientific significances. On one hand, neutron stars may act as effective captors of dark matter, and the accumulation of dark matter within them can modify the equation of state of the star, thereby influencing the mass-radius relationship, tidal deformability, and rotational dynamics. On the other hand, such hybrid systems may produce unique observational features, such as modified neutron star cooling curves, anomalous pulsar timing behaviors, and distorted gravitational wave signals. Additionally, exploring the effects of dark matter phase transitions, condensations, and self-interactions within neutron stars through numerical simulations can provide important astrophysical constraints on the properties of dark matter particles. This numerical simulation library aims to build a flexible and efficient computational platform, continuously integrating and developing various numerical solutions for the two-fluid TOV equations. We will constantly update the library to include, but not be limited to: the implementation of different dark matter equations of state, numerical stability treatments for coupled fluid equations, the design of conservation schemes for relativistic multi-fluid systems, and high-dimensional solvers considering rotation and magnetic field extensions. By open-sourcing this toolkit, we hope to promote cross-disciplinary collaboration in theoretical astrophysics, particle physics, and numerical relativity, providing a reliable computational foundation for understanding the nature of dark matter and the internal structure of neutron stars.
